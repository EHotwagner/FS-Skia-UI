Implementation (real, compiles, deterministic): a bounded pre-ready FIFO in
src/SkiaViewer/SkiaViewer.fs `runPersistentWindow`. Native key events captured before the
render pipeline signals ready (first presented frame) are buffered in capture order and
flushed once ready; past the cap (64) the oldest is dropped with a structured Input/Warning
diagnostic (Principle VII — explicit degradation, no silent loss). After ready, dispatch is
direct. Single-threaded Silk windowing => a plain mutable queue on the host edge needs no lock.
