## Method

Before adding markers, the six pre-migration rows were captured byte-exactly from
`src/Controls/Catalog.fs` and `src/Controls/catalog.yml` into
`readiness/parity-fixtures/<file>.<id>.txt`. The parity test in
`tests/Controls.Tests/CatalogTests.fs` asserts, for each of the six controls,
`CatalogGen.renderFSharpRow fact` equals the captured `Catalog.fs.<id>.txt` and
`CatalogGen.renderYamlRow fact` equals the captured `catalog.yml.<id>.txt`. A second test
asserts the committed files' regions are `Current` (on-disk == fresh render), and a third
asserts the fact table agrees with the public `Catalog.supportedControls` rows it generates.
