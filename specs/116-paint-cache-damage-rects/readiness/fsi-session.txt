Feature 116 — FSI / seam exercise transcript (T005)

The damage-set, picture-cache, and offscreen-diagnostic seams live on the INTERNAL
`RetainedRender` module (reached from the test assemblies via
`[<assembly: InternalsVisibleTo("Controls.Tests")>]` / `InternalsVisibleTo("Elmish.Tests")`),
so the authoritative exercise of the drafted seam is the in-assembly Expecto suite, not a
plain `.fsx` (which cannot see internal types). The public `FrameMetrics` shape IS reachable
and is exercised below + through `ControlsElmish.Perf.runScript`.
